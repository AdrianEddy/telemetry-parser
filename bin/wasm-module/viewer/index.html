Telemetry Viewer

Open a camera file to see what it recorded

Gyroscope, accelerometer, lens, exposure, GPS and every other tag telemetry-parser can read, on one timeline with the video.

  • Stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded; the parser runs in this tab.
  • Reads only what it needs. The parser seeks to the file's headers and metadata samples, and the video plays straight from the disk, so a 100 GB clip is never loaded whole.
  • Plays what the browser decodes. RAW and other files without a playable picture still get the timeline and the views.

Reads files from

  • GoPro HERO5 and later
  • Sony XAVC, MXF
  • DJI Avata, Action, O3/O4, Neo
  • Insta360 .insv, .mp4
  • Canon .mp4, .mxf, .crm
  • Blackmagic RAW .braw
  • RED .r3d
  • ARRI .mxf, .ari
  • Z CAM .mov, .zraw
  • Nikon, Freefly, XTRA, Kandao, Vuze
  • MotionCam Pro .mcraw
  • Betaflight blackbox .bbl, .bfl, .csv
  • ArduPilot .bin, .log
  • Gyroflow .gcsv, protobuf
  • Phone sensor apps, WitMotion, CAMM
